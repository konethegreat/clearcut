# Operator agreement: the points to cover

_Kone Tshivhinda & Claude, 27 Oct 2026. **An outline for an attorney to turn into a real agreement. Not legal advice.** It exists because POPIA expects a written agreement when one party processes personal information on behalf of another (the operator relationship, sections 20 and 21: check the exact obligations). Use it only if the operator runs ClearCut for **other** firms; see [README.md](README.md) question 1._

**Parties:** the firm that uploads invoices (the *account holder*, normally the responsible party) and the person running ClearCut (the *operator*).

1. **Subject, purpose and duration.** Process invoices and similar documents only to provide ClearCut to the account holder (read, check, present, export), for as long as the account exists plus the backup period. **No other purpose**, no sale, no use to train anything.
2. **Instructions.** The operator processes only on the account holder's documented instructions (the account's own use of the service: uploading, reviewing, deleting). The operator tells the account holder if an instruction looks unlawful.
3. **What is processed and about whom.** The categories in [RETENTION-AND-DELETION.md](RETENTION-AND-DELETION.md) section 1: suppliers, customers and individuals named on invoices; names, addresses, VAT, bank and identity numbers, amounts, descriptions.
4. **Confidentiality.** Everyone with access is bound by confidentiality: **[who has administrator or host access, and under what]**.
5. **Security measures** (the operator undertakes to keep them): [THREAT-MODEL.md](../THREAT-MODEL.md) and [DEPLOY.md](../DEPLOY.md): per-account separation in the database, encryption in transit, each stored Anthropic key encrypted, the document reader confined, an audit log, disk encryption on the host **[confirm]**, access limited to **[who]**, a dependency audit every week. The operator will keep these or better, and will **say if they change materially**.
6. **Sub-operators.** The account holder agrees to: **Anthropic** (reads the pages of documents, **outside South Africa**, using the account holder's **own** Anthropic key and so under the account holder's own relationship with Anthropic) and **[hosting provider and country]**. The operator names any new sub-operator and gives **[N] days'** notice, and the account holder may object or leave. **[LAWYER: the cross-border transfer ground; Anthropic's data processing terms.]**
7. **The account holder's own key.** The account holder is responsible for its Anthropic key, its limits and its bill. The operator stores it encrypted, uses it only to read that account's documents, never shows it again, and deletes it on request. The operator never uses its own key for the account holder's documents (the service runs in `tenant-only` mode).
8. **Breach notification.** The operator tells the account holder **[without delay / within N hours]** after it becomes aware of unauthorised access to the account's information, saying what happened, what information, what is being done, and what the account holder should do, so that the account holder can meet its own duty to notify **[LAWYER: section 22]**.
9. **Data subject requests.** The operator helps the account holder to answer requests to access, correct or delete (the service lets an account export, correct and delete documents itself). **[Note the gap: there is no search across documents by a person's name.]**
10. **Return and deletion.** At the end the operator **returns** the account's documents (exports) on request and **deletes** them and the account's keys within **[N] days**, and says when backups holding them will expire. The operator keeps only what the law requires (and the content-free audit and cost records, **[state them]**).
11. **Audit and information.** The operator gives the account holder what it needs to check compliance, and allows an audit **[terms, notice, cost]**.
12. **Liability and the AI's limits.** ClearCut's output is **a reading by an AI model, checked by rules**, and may be wrong; the account holder must check figures that matter against the original. **[LAWYER: what the operator warrants and what it limits, including spend: the account holder pays Anthropic directly and sets its own limits.]**
13. **Where the information is.** The operator's servers are in **[country]**; the information is sent to Anthropic outside South Africa as in point 6.
14. **Term, termination, governing law, disputes, contact points.** **[LAWYER.]**
